BALANCE STUDIO

Equation Transformer

Your actions

    Pick up from a pool. Drag onto a pan. Drag off to remove.

    Keyboard controls
    POOL
    IN HAND
    Object list / keyboard selection
    Click units or drag a selection rectangle. Drag selected units off a pan.

    Your equation

    Linear equations with whole-number objects and a positive whole-number solution. Examples: 2x + 3 = 11, 3(x − 2) = 12.

    Move the mathematics

    Click or hold a pool to collect weights or balloons. Right-click the same pool to return units. Drag your collected objects onto either pan.

    Click individual objects to select them, or drag an empty area around several. Drag selected objects off a pan to remove them. Bring a balloon onto a weight on the same pan: equal opposite quantities attach, hover, and drift away.

    Through 20, quantities stay countable in groups of ten, five, and ones. Above 20, a single labeled weight or balloon grows with the magnitude. Click or hold this large object to pick units from it; right-click returns selected units. Then drag away the amount selected. Drag a rectangle around it to select the whole quantity.

    To divide a balanced equation into whole groups, drag an x box toward the equal-groups target when it appears. A partial change on one pan is allowed: the balance tips until you match it.

    Undo restores the previous objects. Escape clears your hand. Keyboard controls are available at the bottom. Custom equations support positive whole-number x values, signed whole-number constants up to 500, and up to eight x boxes per side. Inequality-solving and fractional objects are reserved for later.
